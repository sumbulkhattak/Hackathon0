# Scheduled Task: Weekly Performance Report

> **Task ID:** weekly-performance
> **Schedule:** Every Monday at 9:00 AM IST
> **Output:** `Plans/WEEKLY-REPORT_{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`
> **Status:** ACTIVE

---

## Execution

1. Aggregate daily reports from the past 7 days
2. Calculate weekly totals: revenue, orders, returns, new customers
3. Identify week-over-week trends
4. Highlight top 5 products and underperformers
5. Summarize customer satisfaction (inquiry resolution rate, avg response time)
6. Flag any unresolved escalations
7. Generate recommendations for the coming week
8. Save to `Plans/`

## Report Sections

1. **Executive Summary** — 3-5 bullet points
2. **Revenue & Orders** — with comparison chart data
3. **Product Performance** — best sellers, slow movers
4. **Customer Service** — inquiries handled, SLA compliance
5. **Inventory Health** — stock alerts, restocks completed
6. **Recommendations** — 2-3 actionable suggestions
