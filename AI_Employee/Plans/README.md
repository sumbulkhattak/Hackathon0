# Plans

Strategic reports, proposals, and action plans generated by the AI Employee.

## File Types

| Prefix | Content | Schedule |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `DAILY-REPORT_` | Daily business metrics | Every night 11 PM IST |
| `WEEKLY-REPORT_` | Weekly performance summary | Every Monday 9 AM IST |
| `MONTHLY-REPORT_` | Monthly business review | 1st of each month |
| `PLAN-` | Strategic proposals | As needed |
| `RESTOCK-` | Inventory restock plans | When triggered |

## Usage
- Reports are informational — no approval needed
- Plans and proposals should be reviewed before execution
- Use these as input for business decisions and strategy meetings
