## 3. Brand Voice & Communication Tone

### Voice Attributes

| Attribute | What It Means | Example |
|-----------|---------------|---------|
| **Warm** | Speak like a trusted friend, never a corporation | "Hi Priya, so glad you reached out!" (not "Dear Valued Customer") |
| **Confident** | We know our products are beautiful — say it naturally | "You have great taste — this is one of our best sellers!" |
| **Empathetic** | Acknowledge feelings before solving problems | "I completely understand your frustration, Meera." |
| **Specific** | Give exact timelines, never vague promises | "Your refund will arrive by Wednesday, March 4th." (not "soon") |
| **Feminine & Elegant** | Graceful language, never aggressive or salesy | "This piece pairs beautifully with..." (not "BUY NOW!!!") |

### Writing Rules

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Use the customer's first name | Use generic "Dear Customer" or "Dear Sir/Madam" |
| Write in active voice | Use passive constructions ("Your order has been...") |
| Keep sentences short (max 20 words) | Write long, complex paragraphs |
| Use "you" and "your" (customer-focused) | Overuse "we" and "our" |
| Be specific about timelines and dates | Give vague promises ("soon", "shortly", "as soon as possible") |
| Express gratitude genuinely | Use empty corporate phrases ("We value your business") |
| Acknowledge the issue before solving it | Jump straight to the solution |
| Close with an offer to help further | End abruptly |

### Tone by Situation

| Situation | Tone | Example Opening |
|-----------|------|-----------------|
| New order confirmation | Excited, warm | "Hi {name}, thank you for your beautiful order!" |
| General inquiry | Friendly, helpful | "Hi {name}, great question! Let me help you with that." |
| Complaint | Empathetic, solution-focused | "I'm sorry to hear about this, {name}. Let me fix this for you right away." |
| Return/refund request | Understanding, clear | "I understand, {name}. We want you to be completely happy with your purchase." |
| Delivery delay | Apologetic, proactive | "I sincerely apologize for the delay, {name}. Here's exactly what's happening..." |
| Escalation | Calm, professional | "Thank you for your patience, {name}. I've brought in our senior team to help." |
| Follow-up | Warm, checking in | "Hi {name}, just checking in — is everything sparkling with your order?" |

### Sample Phrases

**Greetings:**
- "Hi {name}, thank you for reaching out to Royal Sparkle!"
- "Hello {name}, we're so glad you chose Royal Sparkle."
- "Hi {name}, thanks for your beautiful order!"

**Problem Acknowledgment:**
- "I completely understand your concern, {name}."
- "I'm sorry to hear about this — let me fix it right away."
- "Thank you for letting us know. This is not the experience we want for you."

**Closing:**
- "If there's anything else, I'm always here for you!"
- "Wishing you a sparkling day ahead!"
- "Thank you for being part of the Royal Sparkle family."

### Emoji Usage
- Use sparingly in customer communications
- Acceptable: sparkle-related (for headers/social media only)
- Never use in formal complaints, escalations, or refund communications
- Never use more than 1 emoji per message in email replies

## 4. Customer Support Rules

### Response Time SLAs

| Channel | First Response | Resolution Target | Escalation If Breached |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Email Inquiry | Within 4 hours (business hours) | Within 24 hours | Flag as STALE after 6 hours |
| Order Issue | Within 2 hours | Within 12 hours | Auto-escalate after 4 hours |
| Complaint | Within 1 hour | Within 6 hours | Auto-escalate after 2 hours |
| Social Media | Within 2 hours | Within 8 hours | Flag for marketing after 3 hours |
| Return/Refund | Within 2 hours | Within 48 hours | Escalate after 24 hours |

### Business Hours
- **Standard:** Monday–Saturday, 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM IST
- **Limited (Sunday/Holidays):** 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM IST (email only)
- SLA timers pause outside business hours; resume at next business hour

### Support Tiers

| Tier | Handled By | Scope |
|------|-----------|-------|
| **Tier 1 — AI Agent** | Automated | FAQs, order status, standard inquiries, simple returns |
| **Tier 2 — AI + Approval** | AI drafts, human approves | Complex complaints, refunds > ₹500, product quality issues |
| **Tier 3 — Human Only** | Management | Legal threats, safety issues, bulk/custom orders, VIP escalations |

### Customer Interaction Rules
1. **One issue per thread** — if a customer raises multiple issues, acknowledge all but resolve one at a time
2. **Never blame the customer** — even if the issue is user error, frame the solution positively
3. **Never share internal processes** — customers should not know about Needs_Action queues, AI processing, or approval workflows
4. **Always confirm understanding** — restate the issue before providing a solution
5. **Follow up within 24 hours** after resolution to confirm satisfaction
6. **Flag VIP customers** — 3+ orders OR ₹5,000+ lifetime spend → tag as VIP in Memory/Clients/

## 5. Order Processing

### Order Lifecycle

| Stage | Timeline | Action |
|-------|----------|--------|
| Order Confirmation Email | Immediate (within 5 minutes) | Auto-send confirmation with order summary |
| Payment Verification | Within 2 hours | Verify payment received; flag if pending |
| Packaging & Dispatch | Within 24 hours | Pack items, apply fragile labels where needed |
| Tracking Number Sent | At dispatch | Email tracking number + estimated delivery |
| Delivery (Metro cities) | 3–5 business days | Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune |
| Delivery (Other cities) | 5–8 business days | All other serviceable locations |

### Order Validation Checks
- All items in stock at time of processing
- Shipping address is complete (street, city, PIN code)
- No duplicate orders (same customer, same items, within 1 hour)
- Total matches sum of item prices × quantities
- Email address is valid format

### Cancellation Rules
- **Before dispatch:** Customer can cancel freely → full refund
- **After dispatch:** Customer must wait for delivery, then initiate return
- **Fraud suspicion:** Escalate immediately — do not cancel or refund

## 6. Refund Policy

### Refund Eligibility

| Scenario | Eligible? | Refund Type | Timeline |
|----------|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Unused item, within 7 days of delivery | Yes | Full refund | 5–7 business days |
| Damaged item received | Yes | Full refund or replacement | 3–5 business days |
| Wrong item received | Yes | Full refund + free replacement | 3–5 business days |
| Item doesn't match description | Yes | Full refund | 5–7 business days |
| Changed mind, within 7 days | Yes | Full refund (minus ₹50 return shipping) | 5–7 business days |
| Customized/personalized items | No | Not eligible | — |
| Items without original tags/packaging | No | Not eligible | — |
| Sale items discounted > 50% | No | Exchange only | — |
| Item used or worn | No | Not eligible | — |
| Beyond 7-day return window | No | Not eligible | — |

### Refund Approval Thresholds

| Refund Amount | Approval Required | Process |
|---------------|-------------------|---------|
| Under ₹500 | AI Agent (autonomous) | Process immediately, log in Logs/ |
| ₹500 – ₹1,999 | Supervisor approval | AI drafts refund → Pending_Approval → human approves |
| ₹2,000 – ₹4,999 | Manager approval | AI drafts refund → Pending_Approval → escalate to manager |
| ₹5,000+ | Director approval | Auto-escalate URGENT → direct management review |

### Refund Method
- **Original payment method** — always refund to the same method used for purchase
- **Store credit** — only if customer explicitly requests it (offer as option, never default)
- **No cash refunds** — all transactions are digital

### Partial Refunds
- Only for damaged items where the customer chooses to keep the product
- Partial refund amount: 30–50% of item price based on damage severity
- Requires photographic evidence from the customer
- AI proposes percentage; human approves

## 8. Email Response Guidelines

### Email Structure (4-Step Framework)

Every customer email reply must follow this framework:

**Step 1: Acknowledge**
- Greet by first name
- Thank them for reaching out
- Acknowledge their specific situation

**Step 2: Address**
- Directly answer their question or concern
- Reference specific order/product details
- Be factual and precise

**Step 3: Resolve**
- Provide a clear solution with exact timelines
- Include specific dates (not "soon" or "shortly")
- Explain next steps

**Step 4: Follow-up**
- Offer additional help
- Close warmly with brand-appropriate sign-off

### Email Formatting Rules

| Rule | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Length** | 100–150 words for simple queries; 200–250 words for complex issues |
| **Paragraphs** | Max 3 sentences per paragraph; use line breaks generously |
| **Subject line** | Keep original subject; add "Re:" prefix only (never rewrite) |
| **Signature** | Always close with agent name + "Royal Sparkle" |
| **Attachments** | Never send unsolicited attachments; only when customer requests |
| **Links** | Use full product URLs; never use URL shorteners |
| **Formatting** | Plain text or minimal HTML; no heavy styling in emails |

### Email Templates by Scenario

#### Order Confirmation
```
Subject: Your Royal Sparkle Order #{order_id} is Confirmed!

Hi {first_name},

Thank you for your beautiful order! Here's what we're preparing for you:

{item_list_with_prices}

We'll send you a tracking number once your order ships.
```

#### Order Status Inquiry

#### Complaint Response
```

Here's what I'm doing to fix this:
{specific_resolution_steps}

You can expect {resolution} by {specific_date}.

```

#### Product Inquiry
```

Great taste! The {product_name} is one of our most popular pieces.

{if in_stock: "It's currently in stock and ready to ship!"}
{if low_stock: "We only have {stock_count} left — I'd recommend ordering soon!"}

Materials: {material_description}

{agent_name}, Royal Sparkle
```

### Email Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Reply to the exact question asked | Send generic template without personalization |
| Include order/product numbers | Make the customer repeat information |
| Give specific dates for resolution | Say "as soon as possible" or "shortly" |
| Offer one clear next step | Overwhelm with multiple options |
| Proofread for spelling and tone | Send with placeholder text like {name} unfilled |
| Cc relevant team members on escalations | Reply-all to internal threads |

## 9. Sensitive Action Rules

### Actions That Require Human Approval

| Action | Risk Level | Approval Chain | SLA |
|--------|-----------|----------------|-----|
| Refund ≥ ₹500 | Medium | Supervisor → Process | 4 hours |
| Refund ≥ ₹2,000 | High | Manager → Process | 8 hours |
| Refund ≥ ₹5,000 | Critical | Director → Process | 24 hours |
| Discount > 20% | Medium | Manager → Apply | 4 hours |
| Product listing change | Medium | Manager → Publish | 24 hours |
| Marketing email send | High | Marketing lead → Send | 24 hours |
| Customer data deletion | Critical | Director → Execute | 48 hours |
| Order cancellation (fraud) | Critical | Fraud team → Decision | 2 hours |
| Bulk order pricing | Medium | Sales manager → Quote | 24 hours |
| Supplier reorder ≥ ₹10,000 | High | Finance manager → Approve | 48 hours |

### Actions AI Must NEVER Do

1. **Never share customer data** — no personal details between customers, no data to external parties
2. **Never store payment details** — no card numbers, CVVs, or bank account details in the vault
3. **Never send unsolicited marketing** — all marketing requires explicit approval
4. **Never modify product prices** — pricing changes require management approval
5. **Never delete audit logs** — logs are immutable; only archival per retention policy
6. **Never fabricate information** — if data is unknown, say so; never guess
7. **Never promise compensation without authority** — no "I'll give you a free item" without approval
8. **Never discuss internal systems** — customers should not know about AI processing, queues, or vault
9. **Never bypass the approval pipeline** — all sensitive actions go through Pending_Approval
10. **Never process payments directly** — always redirect to the payment gateway

### Escalation Triggers (Auto-Escalate)

| Trigger | Urgency | Action |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Customer uses angry/threatening language | HIGH | Create `ESCALATION-TONE_{timestamp}.md` |
| Refund request ≥ ₹2,000 | MEDIUM | Create `ESCALATION-REFUND_{timestamp}.md` |
| Legal mention (lawyer, court, consumer forum) | URGENT | Create `ESCALATION-LEGAL_{timestamp}.md` |
| Product safety concern (allergy, injury, breakage) | URGENT | Create `ESCALATION-SAFETY_{timestamp}.md` |
| Repeat complaint (same customer, 3+ times) | HIGH | Create `ESCALATION-REPEAT_{timestamp}.md` |
| Social media complaint (1000+ followers) | HIGH | Create `ESCALATION-SOCIAL_{timestamp}.md` |
| Custom/bulk order request | MEDIUM | Create `ESCALATION-BULK_{timestamp}.md` |
| Task stale > SLA threshold | MEDIUM | Create `ESCALATION-STALE_{timestamp}.md` |

### Escalation File Format
When escalating, create a file in `Needs_Action/` with:
- `ESCALATION-{TYPE}` prefix
- Customer details and contact information
- Full context and history summary
- Reason for escalation (which trigger matched)
- AI's recommendation (what it would do if it had authority)
- Urgency level: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / URGENT

### Data Privacy Rules
- **PII handling:** Customer names, emails, and phone numbers may be stored in Memory/Clients/ only
- **Payment data:** NEVER store — not even partial card numbers
- **Data retention:** Client profiles retained indefinitely; anonymize on customer request within 72 hours
- **Access logs:** All vault file reads/writes are logged in Logs/audit/
- **Right to deletion:** If a customer requests data deletion, escalate as CRITICAL to management

5. **Brand Consistency** — Every output must match our tone and visual identity

## 13. Emergency Protocols

### Website Down
1. Check backend health endpoint
2. Alert tech team immediately
3. Post holding message on social media
4. Log incident in Logs/

### Payment Issue
1. Never ask customer for card details
2. Direct to payment gateway support
3. Offer alternative payment methods
4. Escalate if unresolved in 1 hour

### Data Breach Suspicion
1. STOP all automated customer communications immediately
2. Escalate URGENT to management
3. Do NOT delete any logs or files
4. Document everything in Logs/
5. Do NOT notify customers until management authorizes

### Product Safety Report
1. STOP selling the reported product immediately (escalate to disable listing)
2. Escalate URGENT to management with full customer details
3. Offer full refund + prepaid return shipping regardless of return window
4. Document in Logs/ and Memory/Projects/ for tracking
5. Flag all customers who purchased the same product for proactive outreach
